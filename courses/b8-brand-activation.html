From brand to the world, on-brand 6 min live

Activation is taking the finished system into real touchpoints - the site hero, the launch announcement, social posts, product copy, the first onboarding email - and keeping them all recognisably one brand. The brand GPT is the hub: because it holds the whole system and the message house, every touchpoint it drafts starts on-brand. The system is the centre; the touchpoints are the spokes.

LiveWhy activation runs through the brand GPT3 min▶

Activation is where sameness usually creeps back in. A launch is busy, deadlines are tight, and someone opens a blank chat and asks for "a punchy launch post" with no brief - and out comes the category average, in a voice that is not Cadence. The brand GPT prevents exactly that:

  • The hub holds the system. Positioning, voice, and the message house are already loaded, so the first draft is on-brand, not generic.
  • Every spoke starts from the same centre. Site, social, email, and product copy all trace to one narrative - so they read as one brand, not four freelancers.
  • You still own the last step. Human sign-off, proof-check, and IP-check before anything goes public. The GPT drafts; you decide what ships.

Self-studyActivation vs campaign - keep the line clear2 min read▶

They blur in practice, so hold the distinction: activation is making the brand real at each touchpoint in its own voice - the enduring expression. A campaign is a time-bound push to an audience with a goal and a budget. The hero line and the "about" blurb are activation; the two-week paid launch with A/B tested ads is a campaign. Branding owns the first so the second always has something true and distinctive to amplify. Get activation right and every future campaign inherits a consistent brand for free.

Templates + the launch moment 5 min live

Two things make activation repeatable. First, reusable on-brand templates - skeletons driven by the system, so the tenth post is as on-brand as the first. Second, a launch narrative that expresses the brand, not just a feature list - people remember a story about being present, not a spec sheet.

LiveTemplates the system fills, and a launch that says something3 min▶
  • Templates carry the system. A good template has the structure fixed and the brand-specific parts as placeholders the brand GPT fills on-voice - so anyone on the team produces on-brand copy without re-briefing the brand every time.
  • The launch expresses the brand. A feature launch that only lists features is forgettable. Frame it around the narrative: Cadence launches not "AI meeting notes" but "you, finally present in every conversation." The feature is the proof; the brand is the point.
Feature is the proof, brand is the point If your launch line would fit any note-taker, it is a feature line. Rewrite it until only Cadence could say it - that is the message house doing its job at the loudest moment the brand ever has.

Part 3 · the link to learn-ai-marketing

The handoff 4 min live

Brand does not run the campaign - marketing and growth do. But a campaign is only on-brand if brand hands over the right package. The handoff is what makes every future campaign inherit Cadence instead of reinventing it.

LiveWhat marketing needs from brand2 min▶

Three things travel from branding to marketing, and if all three go, campaigns stay on-brand without anyone policing them:

  • The brand system - positioning, personality, voice with its "never say" list, and visual direction. The rules of the road.
  • The message house - the verified narrative, pillars, and proof from b7. What the campaign is allowed to claim.
  • The assets + the brand GPT - the on-brand starting templates and the assistant loaded with all of the above, so the marketing team drafts on-system from day one.

Hand over those three and a campaign built in the AI + Marketing course starts on-brand by default. Skip them, and the campaign quietly drifts back to the average - undoing eight sessions of work in a fortnight.

Generate a small Cadence launch kit ★ 13 min · everyone builds

Produce three on-brand launch pieces through the brand GPT - a site hero line, a launch announcement post, and a product blurb - then check each one against the system before it would ever ship.

Open the Cadence brand GPT (it holds the system from b6 and the message house from b7). If you are in a fresh chat, paste the one-page system and the verified house first.

Ask for a site hero line - the narrative, not the feature - plus a supporting sub-line. Get two or three options to react to.

Ask for a launch announcement post that frames the change in the customer's life, with the feature as proof, in Cadence's voice.

Ask for a short product blurb - the app-store / footer "about Cadence" copy - drawn straight from the message house.

Check each against the system: Is it in voice? Does it hit a "never say" word? Does every claim trace to verified proof? Is there any IP or likeness risk? Fix, then keep the kit.

★ The launch-kit prompt (paste to your Cadence brand GPT)Using Cadence's brand system and verified message house, draft a small launch kit. Stay on-voice (calm, capable, quietly on our side). Never say "revolutionary", "seamless", "game-changer". Use only proof from the house - invent nothing. 1) Site hero: 2-3 options for a headline + sub-line. Lead with the presence narrative, not "AI notes". 2) Launch announcement: one short post that frames the change in the customer's life; the feature is the proof, not the headline. 3) Product blurb: a 3-4 sentence "about Cadence" for a footer or app store. Label anything I need to verify with [VERIFY].

Build one reusable on-brand template ★ 8 min · build your own

One template beats a hundred one-off posts. Build a reusable skeleton with placeholders that the brand GPT fills on-voice - so the next launch post, the next feature note, is on-brand before anyone touches it.

Pick one recurring touchpoint - a feature-announcement post is a good first template. Note its fixed structure (hook, the change, the proof, the call to act).

Ask the brand GPT to turn that structure into a reusable template with clearly marked placeholders like {feature}, {customer benefit}, {proof point}, {CTA} - and on-voice connective copy around them.

Test it: fill the placeholders for one real Cadence feature and check the result is on-brand and true. Adjust the fixed copy until it holds for any feature.

Save it with the launch kit. This template plus the brand GPT is the reusable engine you hand to marketing - and reuse yourself in b9 and b10.

★ The template promptUsing Cadence's brand system and message house, build a REUSABLE template for a feature- announcement post. Keep the structure fixed and mark the brand-specific parts as placeholders: {feature}, {customer benefit}, {proof point}, {CTA}. Write the connective copy around the placeholders in Cadence's voice, so anyone can drop in a new feature and get an on-brand post. Never say "revolutionary", "seamless", "game-changer". Then show it filled in once for a real feature as an example.
A template is the system, made portable The fixed copy is your brand voice baked in; the placeholders are the only thing that changes per post. That is how you scale on-brand output without re-briefing the brand every single time - and exactly what marketing needs to stay consistent at campaign volume.
