# Official course map - learn-ai-branding-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Branding - how brand builders embrace AI
**Scope:** Corporate brand-building, broad: brand strategy, positioning, personality/archetype, naming + verbal identity/voice, visual identity DIRECTION, brand guidelines + consistency at scale, messaging architecture, activation, brand monitoring/health, governance + IP. EXCLUDES final visual-design craft (a future design course) and campaign execution (that is the sibling learn-ai-marketing course). Branding = the enduring brand; marketing = campaigns; design = visual craft.
**Arc:** Running-project - the builder track builds ONE brand foundation end to end for **"Cadence"** (the AI note-taker from learn-ai-marketing; this course is the prequel to its campaign). Two-track (a1-a6 leader, b1-b10 builder).
**Coverage bar:** ~80% of mapped sources' working content per session; certificates/paid tooling stay official.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (built direct from verified sources, no learner-notes step).

## Market context (verify before delivery - moves fast)

- The AI-era paradox: generative AI floods every category with cheap, samey content, so a distinctive, well-documented brand is the differentiator, not a nice-to-have.
- Documented brand guidelines (especially VERBAL/voice, with "never say" rules + sample copy) are now the mechanism that lets a team use AI at scale WITHOUT diluting the brand - the single biggest 2026 shift.
- "Living"/adaptive identities and brand GPTs (custom instructions trained on the brand) are emerging.

## Hard rails (bake into every relevant page)

- **"AI assists, humans own the brand soul and the claim."** AI accelerates exploration + production; strategy, meaning, and final approval stay human.
- **The sameness warning:** AI homogenizes; a documented brand system (esp. verbal identity + "never say") is the antidote. This is the course's spine.
- **IP/likeness rail:** AI-generated names/logos/images raise trademark, copyright, and right-of-publicity/likeness risk - clear before use, human review, check tool commercial terms.
- **Disclosure rail:** FTC/ASA - disclose AI + endorsements where required; no deceptive brand claims.
