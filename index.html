Learn AI + Branding with Phoebe

Two tracks for brand builders who embrace AI

A leader track that teaches brand and marketing leaders to steer AI without diluting the brand - and a builder track where you build one brand foundation end to end for a sample company, Cadence, using AI at every step: positioning, personality, naming and voice, visual direction, guidelines, and messaging. In a world flooded with AI sameness, a documented brand is your only real moat, and this is how you build one AI can scale without breaking.

The one rule this whole course runs on: AI assists, humans own the brand soul and the claim. AI floods every category with sameness - a documented brand system (especially your voice and your "never say") is the antidote, and IP, likeness, and disclosure always get a human check.
